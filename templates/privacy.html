Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address and a hashed version of your password. When you log expenses, we store the amount, category, date, and any description you provide. We do not collect payment information or any data beyond what is needed to operate the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide you with expense tracking functionality — displaying your transactions, generating summaries, and filtering by date or category. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the service.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. Passwords are never stored in plain text. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal data with third parties. We use Google Fonts to load typefaces; that request is made directly from your browser to Google's servers and is subject to Google's own privacy policy. No other third-party services receive your data.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us. We will respond to all privacy-related enquiries within a reasonable timeframe.
